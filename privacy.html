TRAILFOG

PRIVACY POLICY

TrailFog is made by Motionz Studios. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you control it.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Your rides are stored on your phone.
  • Trails only leave your phone if you join a group or the world leaderboard.
  • Privacy zones keep chosen places, like your home, completely off the server.
  • No account is needed to ride solo. To use groups and leaderboards you sign in with Apple or Google, and you pick a rider name.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.

What we collect and why

Location

When you tap Start Ride, TrailFog records your GPS position, including while your phone is locked, so it can clear the fog along your route. Recording stops when you tap Stop. Rides are saved on your device. If you are in a group or on the world leaderboard, your ride points (outside your privacy zones) are uploaded so that group members can see your trails and so leaderboard distance and new ground can be calculated and checked for cheating.

Photos

Photos are only taken when you tap the camera button. They are saved on your device with the place they were taken. If you are in a group, a smaller copy of each photo outside your privacy zones is shared with that group.

Rider name and ID

The app creates a random rider ID on your phone. If you use groups or leaderboards, your ID and the rider name you choose are stored on our server and shown to group members or on the leaderboard.

Account (Sign in with Apple or Google)

If you sign in, we receive an account ID and the email address from Apple or Google. With Apple you can choose to hide your email, and we then only get a private relay address. We use this only to sign you in and keep your rider, groups and shared trails if you change phones. We never email you marketing and never show your email to other riders.

Reports

If you report a rider, we store the report and its reason so we can review it.

Map images

Map images are downloaded from map providers (currently OpenTopoMap and Esri). Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and the map area being shown, but not your account or ride history.

Where it's stored

Shared data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it on our behalf. We keep it until you delete it or leave, or until it's no longer needed for the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a ride in the Rides tab, and it's removed from the server too.
  • Leave your group or the world leaderboard to remove your shared trails.
  • Add privacy zones in Settings at any time. Existing shared trails are re-uploaded without those areas.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete my account. This removes your rider, everything you shared and your sign-in from our server.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for help deleting it, by emailing andystevo958@gmail.com with your rider name.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone.

If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Children

TrailFog is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
