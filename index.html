Help

The fog isn't clearing behind me

Allow location Always (iPhone: Settings → TrailFog → Location → Always, and turn on Precise Location). On Android, allow location All the time and turn off battery saving for TrailFog. Then tap Start Ride before you set off.

My trail jumps to the wrong place

TrailFog ignores weak GPS fixes, so trails can take a few seconds to start under trees or in valleys. Keep the phone where it can see the sky if you can.

How do groups work?

Open the Group tab, create a group and share the 6-letter code with your mates. Everyone in the group sees each other's trails and photo pins. Leave the group at any time from the same tab.

How do I hide where I live?

Go to Settings → Privacy zones and add a zone around your home. Nothing recorded inside a zone is ever uploaded, and the zone itself is never shown to anyone.

Reporting a rider or a photo

Tap a rider on the leaderboard and choose Report, or email us. We review reports and remove riders or content that break the rules.

Deleting your data

Delete a ride from the Rides tab and it's removed from the server too. Leaving your group and the world leaderboard removes your shared trails. To delete your account and everything you shared, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account, or email us with your rider name.

New phone?

Install TrailFog, open Settings → Account and sign in with the same Apple or Google account. Your rider name, group, leaderboard place and shared trails come back.

Ride safe

Only ride where you're allowed to, follow local laws and trail rules, and respect landowners and other trail users. Don't use your phone while riding.
